# Korean Fiction Craft Contract

Use as reusable craft guidance, never as canonical story state.

- Chapter beats may alternate action and sequel reaction -> dilemma -> decision.
- Escalation changes pressure, options, knowledge, or relationship state; repetition without changed state is not escalation.
- Prefer yes-but / no-and consequences when useful; do not mechanically ban clean successes.
- Dialogue tracks want, withheld information, tactic, status, relationship-specific address, honorific/banmal, and pressure-induced register changes.
- Psychic distance must be deliberate and stable unless a transition is intentional.
- Exposition earns its place by affecting present action, choice, interpretation, or suspense.
- Openings establish live pressure/question quickly; endings change state, expectation, or decision rather than merely stop.
- Style review measures sentence/paragraph tendencies and author-specific fingerprints; labels like lyrical are insufficient alone.
- Flag cliches and generic AI-shaped prose while preserving deliberate roughness, ornament, repetition, or idiolect supported by author preference/sample.
- Narrative texture audit flags excessive tidiness, single-track causality, over-explained theme, moral neatness, and formulaic resolution. It never automatically injects ambiguity or chronology tricks.
